Privacy Policy for Last Delivery

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Last Delivery is developed and published by Pica XR Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Last Delivery.

Information We Process and Why

Last Delivery processes information needed to run the game, save your progress, and verify access to purchased content. Processing includes using information temporarily, even when it is not saved or sent to a developer-operated server.

Meta Account, User ID, and Purchase Information

Last Delivery uses the Meta Platform SDK in the context of your signed-in Meta account to check app entitlement and ownership of the Casino DLC. Meta associates these checks with your account and its platform user identifier (User ID). The game's Casino DLC code does not separately request your numeric User ID or store it in game saves; it requests the current account's entitlement and purchases.

  • App entitlement result: used to verify that the current account is entitled to use Last Delivery before checking DLC access.
  • Purchase information: the application receives purchase records from Meta and checks the product identifier (SKU), purchase type, and expiration status to determine whether the Casino DLC is owned.
  • Checkout result and store errors: used to refresh ownership after checkout and show relevant purchase errors to the player.

These checks enable access to purchased content and restore access when you return to the game. The Casino DLC system holds the access result in memory during the current session and rechecks ownership when needed. It does not write the purchase list to game saves or send it to a developer-operated server.

Purchases

Purchases of Last Delivery and its DLC are processed by Meta. When you choose to buy the Casino DLC, the game opens Meta checkout. Pica XR Studio does not receive your payment card details. Clearing game data does not delete the purchase records held by Meta or cancel ownership of purchased content.

Local Game Data

The game saves progress and settings on your device, including inventory, in-game currency, story progress, and language preferences. These files allow the game to restore your progress and preferences between sessions and remain until they are overwritten, reset, or removed. Device or platform backup features, if enabled, may retain or restore copies under their own settings and policies.

Support and Privacy Requests

If you email Pica XR Studio, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to answer your question or handle your request. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and store messages. We retain it only as needed to handle the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Sharing and Meta Platform Services

The entitlement and purchase features communicate with Meta through its Platform SDK. We do not sell the information described in this policy or use the Casino DLC account and purchase checks for advertising.

Meta also processes account, device, transaction, usage, and diagnostic information for its own platform services. Meta's independent processing and retention are governed by the Meta Privacy Policy. This does not replace the description above of how Last Delivery uses the results and purchase information returned by Meta.

How to Request Data Deletion

Users in any country can request deletion of personal information held by Pica XR Studio by emailing grigorovalexey95@gmail.com with the subject Last Delivery - Data Deletion Request. You can use the same address to ask what information we hold about you or request a correction.

Describe the information you want deleted, such as earlier support correspondence. Do not send passwords or payment card details. If clarification is needed to locate your information or verify the request, we will ask only for what is necessary. We will delete information we hold about you without undue delay, subject to applicable requirements, and confirm the outcome. If we hold no relevant information, or must retain specific information, we will explain this and the reason.

To remove local game saves, clear Last Delivery's application data using your device's application or storage controls. This resets local progress and settings. If a platform backup exists, manage or delete it separately through the platform's backup controls to prevent restoration. Contact us if you need help locating these controls.

Pica XR Studio cannot delete records independently held by Meta, including your Meta account, store transactions, or platform backups. For these records, use Meta's account and privacy controls or contact Meta support. You can still contact us to request deletion of any information held by Pica XR Studio.

Children
