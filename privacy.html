Privacy Policy for Last Delivery

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Last Delivery is developed and published by Pica XR Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how that information is collected, why it is used, how it is stored, and how users can request deletion of their personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Last Delivery collects and processes a limited amount of information necessary to operate the game, save gameplay progress, and verify access to purchased content.

The information collected by Last Delivery includes:

  • Meta Horizon in-app purchase and entitlement information;
  • purchase verification results and checkout information;
  • locally stored gameplay progress and settings;
  • information voluntarily provided through support emails.

Last Delivery does not maintain a separate developer-operated account database or a server containing Meta Horizon purchase records.

2. Data Collected from Meta Horizon

Last Delivery uses Meta Horizon Platform services and the Meta Platform SDK to verify ownership of the Casino Access add-on (product identifier: casino_unlock).

When a player checks ownership of the add-on, restores purchased content, or completes a purchase, Last Delivery collects (receives) in-app purchase and entitlement data directly from Meta Horizon through the Meta Platform SDK.

Specific Data Collected

Last Delivery collects the following purchase-related information from Meta Horizon:

  • Product identifier (SKU): identifies the purchased add-on, including casino_unlock. This allows the game to recognize which content the purchase relates to.
  • Purchase and entitlement status: indicates whether the current Meta Horizon account owns the purchased content. The game uses this result to determine whether Casino Access should be unlocked.
  • Purchase verification results: information returned by Meta Horizon when the game requests confirmation of ownership. These results are used to restore access to previously purchased content.
  • Checkout results: information indicating whether an attempted in-app purchase was completed successfully. This is used to update access to Casino Access.
  • Purchase-related error information: error results returned by Meta Horizon when a purchase or ownership verification fails. These results are used to inform the player and handle unsuccessful purchase attempts.

The Meta Horizon purchase records accessed by the game can also contain purchase type and entitlement expiration information. Where those fields are returned and read by the game, they are used only to identify the purchase type and check whether the entitlement remains valid.

How Data Is Collected

This information is collected automatically when Last Delivery communicates with Meta Horizon Platform services through the Meta Platform SDK.

The information comes directly from Meta Horizon purchase and entitlement responses. Players do not need to manually enter personal information into the game to verify their purchase.

Why This Data Is Collected

The purchase and entitlement information is collected exclusively to:

  • identify the Casino Access add-on;
  • verify ownership of purchased content;
  • unlock the casino after a valid purchase;
  • restore access to previously purchased content;
  • confirm the result of an in-app purchase;
  • handle purchase and entitlement verification errors.

Without this information, the game cannot reliably determine whether the current player owns Casino Access.

3. Storage and Retention of Purchase Data

The purchase and entitlement information received from Meta Horizon is processed temporarily by Last Delivery while the game is running.

The game uses the ownership verification result in memory to determine whether purchased content is available during the current gameplay session.

Pica XR Studio does not transmit Meta Horizon purchase or entitlement records to a developer-operated server. The game does not maintain a developer-operated database of users' purchases or entitlements.

Temporary purchase verification information held in memory is not retained by a developer-operated system after the gameplay session ends.

When necessary, Last Delivery requests updated purchase or entitlement information from Meta Horizon again.

4. Use and Sharing of Purchase Information

Last Delivery uses the collected Meta Horizon purchase information only to provide in-app purchase functionality, verify purchased content, and unlock Casino Access.

Pica XR Studio does not use this information for:

  • advertising;
  • profiling;
  • marketing;
  • selling personal information;
  • unrelated analytics or tracking.

Pica XR Studio does not share purchase or entitlement information with third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

5. Payment Information

Purchases of Last Delivery and its add-ons are processed through Meta Horizon's payment and checkout services.

When a player purchases Casino Access, the payment is handled by Meta.

Pica XR Studio does not collect, receive, or store credit card numbers, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Payment transactions and purchase records independently maintained by Meta are subject to Meta's own privacy and retention policies.

6. Local Game Data

Last Delivery collects and stores gameplay information locally on the user's device to save game progress and preferences.

The locally collected information includes:

  • story and mission progress;
  • inventory information;
  • in-game currency balances;
  • gameplay settings;
  • language preferences.

This information is used to restore gameplay progress and user preferences between sessions.

Local game data is stored on the user's device and remains there until it is overwritten, reset, or deleted.

Platform backup services, if enabled, may independently retain copies of this information according to Meta's backup policies.

7. Support Information

If a user contacts Pica XR Studio by email, we collect the email address, message content, and any information or attachments voluntarily included in that communication.

This information is used only to respond to questions, provide support, or process privacy and deletion requests.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as necessary to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Meta Horizon Platform Services

Last Delivery communicates with Meta Horizon Platform services to access purchase verification and entitlement functionality.

Meta independently collects and processes certain account, device, transaction, usage, and diagnostic information when users interact with its platform.

Meta's independent data processing is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Pica XR Studio does not control Meta's independently maintained account or transaction databases.

Deletion of Meta Horizon Purchase Data

Last Delivery does not maintain a developer-operated database containing Meta Horizon purchase or entitlement records.

Purchase verification information received from Meta Horizon is used temporarily during gameplay and is not stored in a developer-operated database.

As a result, Pica XR Studio may have no separately stored purchase information to delete.

Purchase history, payment transactions, Meta account information, and other records independently maintained by Meta must be managed through Meta's own privacy and account controls or Meta support.

Deletion of Local Game Data

Users can delete locally stored Last Delivery game data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application using their Meta Quest device's application or storage controls.

This removes locally stored game progress and settings. If platform cloud backup is enabled, users may also need to manage or remove the corresponding backup through the platform's backup controls.

Deleting local game data does not delete purchase transactions maintained independently by Meta.

10. Advertising

Last Delivery does not contain third-party advertising.

Information collected through Meta Horizon in-app purchase features is not used for targeted advertising.

11. Children's Privacy

Last Delivery is intended for users who meet the applicable minimum age requirements of Meta Quest and the age rating assigned to the application.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
