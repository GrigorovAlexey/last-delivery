Last Delivery

A single-player VR adventure for Meta Quest.

About the Game

You arrive in a futuristic city to visit an old friend, only to discover that he has disappeared. No one seems to know where he went, and there are almost no clues.

To continue the search, you need money, so you take a job as a courier. Prepare and deliver pizza across the city, meet unusual characters, explore its districts and slowly uncover the mystery behind your friend's disappearance.

What starts as a simple side job gradually turns into a dangerous journey through a city where the neon streets hide far more than they seem.
