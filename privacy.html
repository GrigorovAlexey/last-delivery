Privacy Policy for Last Delivery

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Last Delivery is developed and published by Pica XR Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Last Delivery.

Information We Process and Why

Last Delivery processes information needed to run the game, save your progress, and verify access to purchased content. Processing may include using information temporarily, even when it is not saved or sent to a developer-operated server.

Meta Purchase and Entitlement Information

Last Delivery uses Meta Horizon Platform services to verify ownership of purchased content, including the Casino Access add-on (SKU: casino_unlock).

  • Purchase and entitlement information: used to determine whether the current user owns the Casino Access add-on and should have access to the casino content.
  • Checkout result and store errors: used to refresh access after a purchase and display relevant purchase errors.

This information is used only to verify access to purchased content. Last Delivery does not use this information for advertising, profiling, or selling personal data.

Purchase and entitlement information is not stored on a developer-operated server. The game checks ownership through Meta Horizon Platform services when necessary.

Purchases

Purchases of Last Delivery and its add-ons are processed by Meta. When you choose to buy the Casino Access add-on, the game opens Meta checkout. Pica XR Studio does not receive or store your payment card information.

Deleting the game or clearing local application data does not remove purchase records maintained by Meta or cancel ownership of purchased content.

Local Game Data

Last Delivery saves game progress and settings locally on your device. This may include inventory, in-game currency, story progress, and language preferences.

These files are used to restore your progress and preferences between sessions. They remain on your device until they are overwritten, reset, or removed.

Device or platform backup features, if enabled, may retain or restore copies according to their own settings and policies.

Support and Privacy Requests

If you contact Pica XR Studio by email, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide.

This information is used only to respond to your message or handle your request. We retain correspondence only as necessary to provide support and comply with applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Sharing and Meta Platform Services

Purchase and entitlement features communicate with Meta through Meta Horizon Platform services.

Pica XR Studio does not sell the information described in this policy and does not use purchase or entitlement information for advertising or profiling.

Meta may independently process account, device, transaction, usage, and diagnostic information as part of its platform services. Meta's processing and retention of this information is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

You may also use this email address to ask what personal information Pica XR Studio holds about you or to request a correction.

Please describe the information you want deleted. Do not send passwords or payment card information.

If Pica XR Studio holds relevant personal information, it will be deleted where applicable and subject to legal requirements. If no relevant information is held, you will be informed.

To remove locally stored game data, you can delete or clear Last Delivery's application data using your device's application or storage controls. This will reset locally stored progress and settings.

Pica XR Studio cannot delete records independently maintained by Meta, including Meta account information, store transactions, purchases, or platform backups. For these records, use Meta's privacy and account controls or contact Meta support.
