Privacy Policy for Last Delivery

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Last Delivery is developed and published by Pica XR Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information Last Delivery processes, why it is processed, how it is used, and how users can request deletion of information controlled by Pica XR Studio.

Information We Process

Last Delivery processes only information necessary to operate the game, save local game progress, and provide access to purchased content.

The game does not maintain a developer-operated account database and does not store Meta Horizon purchase records on a developer-operated server.

Meta Horizon In-App Purchase and Add-on Data

Last Delivery uses Meta Horizon Platform services to provide and verify access to the Casino Access add-on with the product identifier casino_unlock.

When Last Delivery checks ownership of the add-on or completes a purchase, Meta Horizon Platform services may provide the game with information related to that purchase or entitlement.

The specific information used by Last Delivery may include:

  • Product identifier (SKU): used to identify the purchased product, including casino_unlock.
  • Purchase or entitlement status: used to determine whether the current user owns Casino Access and whether the casino content should be unlocked.
  • Purchase status: used to determine whether a purchase was successfully completed.
  • Purchase type: used where provided by Meta to identify the type of purchased content.
  • Expiration information: used where provided by Meta to determine whether an entitlement is currently valid.
  • Checkout result and purchase-related error information: used to refresh access after checkout and to display relevant purchase errors to the user.

How Purchase Information Is Used

The purchase and entitlement information described above is used only to:

  • verify ownership of Casino Access;
  • unlock purchased casino content;
  • restore access to previously purchased content;
  • process and confirm purchases made through Meta Horizon;
  • display purchase-related errors when necessary.

Pica XR Studio does not use purchase or entitlement information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or selling personal information.

Last Delivery does not store the Meta Horizon purchase list or entitlement records in a developer-operated server or database. The game checks ownership through Meta Horizon Platform services when needed.

Any temporary access or ownership result used by the game is used only to provide the purchased content during gameplay.

Payment Information

Purchases of Last Delivery and its add-ons are processed by Meta. When a user chooses to purchase Casino Access, the game uses Meta Horizon's checkout system.

Pica XR Studio does not receive, collect, or store payment card numbers, bank account information, or other payment credentials. Payment processing is handled by Meta.

Deleting Last Delivery or clearing the application's local data does not cancel a purchase or remove transaction records independently maintained by Meta.

Local Game Data

Last Delivery stores game progress and settings locally on the user's device. Local game data may include:

  • story progress;
  • inventory;
  • in-game currency;
  • game settings;
  • language preferences.

This information is used only to restore game progress and preferences between gameplay sessions.

Local game data remains on the device until it is overwritten, reset, or deleted by the user.

Platform backup functionality, if enabled by the user or platform, may independently retain or restore copies according to Meta's own backup settings and policies.

Support Information

If a user contacts Pica XR Studio by email, Pica XR Studio receives the email address, message, and any information or attachments the user voluntarily provides.

This information is used only to respond to the user's question, provide support, or process a privacy request.

Support correspondence is retained only as necessary to handle the request and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and Meta Horizon Platform Services

Last Delivery communicates with Meta Horizon Platform services when processing purchases and verifying ownership of purchased content.

Pica XR Studio does not sell personal information and does not share purchase or entitlement information with third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

Meta may independently process account, device, transaction, usage, and diagnostic information as part of operating the Meta Horizon platform. Meta's independent processing is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Purchase history, transaction records, Meta account information, and other records independently maintained by Meta are controlled by Meta. Requests concerning those records must be made through Meta's privacy, account, or support tools.

Deleting Local Game Data

Users may delete locally stored Last Delivery game data by clearing the application's data or removing the application using the storage or application controls available on their Meta Quest device.

This removes locally stored game progress and settings. It does not delete purchases or transaction records maintained by Meta.
